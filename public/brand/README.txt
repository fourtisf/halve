Halve brand assets
==================

The mark ("Stack"): two paper bars are the share, the gold bar lifted off the
top is the dividend. 64-unit grid. Bars 48 x 10, radius 3. Top bar lifted 10
units and shifted 6 units right. Never rotate it, never add a gradient, never
close the gap.

Colors
  Ink    #000000  (backgrounds)          Paper  #FAFAFA
  Gold   #E8C170                          Ink on light: #0A0A0A

Files
  svg/halve-mark.svg            paper + gold, for dark backgrounds
  svg/halve-mark-light.svg      ink + gold, for light backgrounds
  svg/halve-mark-mono.svg       one color, inherits currentColor
  svg/halve-mark-mono-gold.svg  one color, gold
  svg/halve-lockup*.svg         mark + wordmark (Inter SemiBold, outlined)
  svg/halve-app-icon.svg        black rounded square (radius 14/64)
  svg/halve-avatar.svg          black circle
  svg/halve-cover.svg           1600 x 900 cover
  png/                          the same at 16 - 1024 px, transparent

Rules
  Clear space: one bar height (10/64 of the mark) on every side.
  Minimum size: 16 px for the mark, 24 px height for the lockup.
  Lockup: wordmark = 0.92 x mark height, gap = 0.27 x mark height,
  caps optically centred on the mark.
